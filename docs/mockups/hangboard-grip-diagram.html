Hangboard grip diagram

The picture above the countdown has to say two things at a glance from a metre away: which fingers and what shape they are in. The one that shipped in May does both abstractly. Below is that one, then four other attempts, each drawn for every finger setting and grip type in the routine editor.

Where the current picture falls short:

  • Pinch looks identical to 4 Finger. There is no thumb anywhere, and the thumb is the whole point of a pinch.
  • Unused fingers just fade. Front 3 and Back 3 differ by one faint shape, and nothing says which end is the index.
  • The side stroke is a symbol, not a finger. Drag and Open Hand are a straight line and a nearly straight line. Full Crimp is an upside-down V that starts at the board face, which is not where a finger starts.
  • The grip type also changes the fingertip corners on the left panel, from round for drag to square for full crimp. Nobody has ever noticed.

Which to build

A is the safe change: same layout, same place, the fixes are all local to GripDiagram.jsx, and nobody has to relearn the picture. It fixes every point in the list above.

C is the one I would pick. The side-on hand is the picture every climber already has of a crimp, the thumb does visible work, and the four lettered pips underneath remove any guessing about fingers. It is the one of the five where the grip is drawn biggest and the thumb's job in a Full Crimp or a Pinch is clearest.

B is the best of the five for which fingers, and the worst for grip type, because a top-down view cannot show a joint angle. D is the honest fallback: no picture to misread, and the cheapest to build, but it turns the timer screen into a form.

Every option keeps the same inputs the timer already passes, plus the phase background colour for the joint marks in A, C and D. None of them changes stored data.

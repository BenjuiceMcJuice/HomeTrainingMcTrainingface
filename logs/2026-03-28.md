# 2026-03-28 — Work Log

## What we did

### Bug fixes
- **Chart bars fixed:** Flash/send/attempt segments now render adjacently (flash solid left, sends 40% opacity next, attempts grey behind) instead of overlapping. Matches the legend correctly.
- **GymLogSheet compact footer:** Difficulty selector reduced to single-row labels (no numbers), notes + date on same row, tighter padding. Gives exercise cards much more scroll space on mobile.
- **Firebase auth: signInWithPopup → signInWithRedirect:** Fixes "missing initial state" and session errors on mobile browsers, in-app browsers (Instagram/WhatsApp), and browsers with strict cookie policies. Page redirects to Google then back — more reliable than popup.

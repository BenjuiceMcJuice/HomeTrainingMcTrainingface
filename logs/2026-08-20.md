## Route A confirmed working on iOS

The one thing left unproven yesterday — whether iOS actually picks the calendar feed up — is now
proven. Ben subscribed on the phone and sent a screenshot of the event detail view.

What the screenshot confirms, beyond "it appeared":

- **The feed installed as its own calendar**, "BetaLog Training", not merged into an existing one.
  That is the `X-WR-CALNAME` we set being honoured.
- **`RRULE` expansion is correct.** iOS reads it back as "Repeats every week on Tuesday, Thursday
  and Saturday" — the `days[]` → `BYDAY` mapping survives the round trip through Apple's parser,
  which was the part most likely to be subtly wrong.
- **`DTSTART` anchoring works.** The event sits at 09:05-09:35 on the right day, so `remindFrom`
  is doing its job and the floating-time decision (no `TZID`) reads correctly on a real device.
- **The `URL` property surfaces** as a tappable betalog.co.uk row on the event.

Not visible in a static screenshot: whether the `VALARM` actually fired at 09:05. The alarm is
`TRIGGER;RELATED=START:PT0S`, so it should fire at the event start rather than before it.

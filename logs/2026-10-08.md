## BTL-B125 — the alcohol-free streak counts whole days

Ben, with the dashboard card reading *4 days* on a Thursday after a Sunday drink: *"Can we change
the alcohol so it's x full days with no booze type measure. As today counts as a whole day."*

The streak counted from today backwards, so a day joined it the moment it had no entry — at
breakfast, with the whole day still to go. Now `calcAlcoholFreeStreak` (`src/lib/stats.js`) counts
finished days only: yesterday backwards, today never. A drink logged today still returns 0, since
the run is over rather than unfinished. Mon, Tue, Wed after a Sunday drink reads *3 days*, and
becomes 4 at midnight.

On the card the week strip fills one bar per whole day, the latest at full strength, and today's
bar — not yet earned — pulses faintly next in line instead of glowing as filled. The `al-bar-glow`
keyframe is replaced by `al-bar-today`. Guide row and health-log spec §5 updated. Tests: the two
counting cases re-stated for the new rule and one added for *drank yesterday → 0*; 120 pass.

Branch `claude/alcohol-streak-full-days`, cache v82. A change to a working screen, so it stops at
the branch for Ben's word.

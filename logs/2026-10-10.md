# 2026-10-10

## BTL-B128 — the shell rows painted over the sheets

Ben, with a History screenshot of yesterday's Flashpoint session open: *"Can't edit the climbing
history as the screen doesn't move around. Check this on all screens if possible and fix."*

**What the screenshot shows.** The session sheet is open, but the app header and the bottom tabs
are drawn *over* it, undimmed: the sheet's title and close button are under the header (only the
date line peeks out), and its Edit / Delete footer is under the tabs. The climbs list scrolls, so
"the screen doesn't move" is the sheet refusing to go anywhere useful — no way to edit, no way to
close except a tap on the header's own buttons.

**Cause.** BTL-B127 made the header and the tabs rows of a flex column, and left their `z-50` on,
from the days they were `sticky` and `fixed`. A flex item with a z-index is a stacking context in
the root context, exactly like a `position: fixed` overlay, and every sheet rendered inside
`<main>` at `fixed inset-0 z-50` sits at the same level — so paint order is tree order, and the
tabs, last in the shell, won over every z-50 sheet; the header, over the top of any sheet tall
enough to reach it (on a phone, where the status bar makes the header taller, the 92vh session
sheet does). Sheets at `z-[60]` and up (climb editor, Settings, Help, Friends, goals, the comp
sheets) were above the rows and fine, which is why most of the app still worked.

The ones at `z-50`, all hit the same way: the session detail sheet (History), the gym logger,
the cardio logger, the drink sheet and the weigh-in sheet (Log), the exercise modal and the two
routine modals (Plan).

**Fix.** No z-index on any shell row — the header, the desktop nav, `BottomTabs`. Nothing can be
under a row except an overlay, and an overlay must win. Three class attributes in `Nav.jsx`, with
the reason in the comments. The sheets are untouched.

**Seen running** in Chromium at 390 × 844 with Firebase faked (`dev/`), by `elementFromPoint` at
each button's centre: before, the session sheet's Edit and Delete at y 778–820 hit the tab bar;
after, they hit the buttons, the header is dimmed under the backdrop and the sheet covers the
tabs. Save Drink, Cancel / Add Exercise, Create Routine, Save Session all reachable at the foot of
their sheets. Desktop at 1100 × 700 unchanged. Build, 937 tests, lint clean (0 errors; the 13
warnings pre-date this). Cache v84 → v85.

**Not seen on a phone.** Ben's screenshot is the before; the after is the same sheet with its
title and X at the top and Edit at the bottom.

Fix of a broken screen, so merged to `main` without asking (CLAUDE.md, *When to merge without

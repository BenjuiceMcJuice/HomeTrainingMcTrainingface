# 2026-10-10

## BTL-B128 — the shell rows painted over the sheets

Ben, with a History screenshot of yesterday's Flashpoint session open: *"Can't edit the climbing
history as the screen doesn't move around. Check this on all screens if possible and fix."*

**What the screenshot shows.** The session sheet is open, but the app header and the bottom tabs
are drawn *over* it, undimmed: the sheet's title and close button are under the header (only the
date line peeks out), and its Edit / Delete footer is under the tabs. The climbs list scrolls, so
"the screen doesn't move" is the sheet refusing to go anywhere useful — no way to edit, no way to
close except a tap on the header's own buttons.

**Cause.** BTL-B127 made the header and the tabs rows of a flex column, and left their `z-50` on,
from the days they were `sticky` and `fixed`. A flex item with a z-index is a stacking context in
the root context, exactly like a `position: fixed` overlay, and every sheet rendered inside
`<main>` at `fixed inset-0 z-50` sits at the same level — so paint order is tree order, and the
tabs, last in the shell, won over every z-50 sheet; the header, over the top of any sheet tall
enough to reach it (on a phone, where the status bar makes the header taller, the 92vh session
sheet does). Sheets at `z-[60]` and up (climb editor, Settings, Help, Friends, goals, the comp
sheets) were above the rows and fine, which is why most of the app still worked.

The ones at `z-50`, all hit the same way: the session detail sheet (History), the gym logger,
the cardio logger, the drink sheet and the weigh-in sheet (Log), the exercise modal and the two
routine modals (Plan).

**Fix.** No z-index on any shell row — the header, the desktop nav, `BottomTabs`. Nothing can be
under a row except an overlay, and an overlay must win. Three class attributes in `Nav.jsx`, with
the reason in the comments. The sheets are untouched.

**Seen running** in Chromium at 390 × 844 with Firebase faked (`dev/`), by `elementFromPoint` at
each button's centre: before, the session sheet's Edit and Delete at y 778–820 hit the tab bar;
after, they hit the buttons, the header is dimmed under the backdrop and the sheet covers the
tabs. Save Drink, Cancel / Add Exercise, Create Routine, Save Session all reachable at the foot of
their sheets. Desktop at 1100 × 700 unchanged. Build, 937 tests, lint clean (0 errors; the 13
warnings pre-date this). Cache v84 → v85.

**Seen on a phone.** Ben, a few minutes after the merge: *"It's the same in the weight logging
section I can't log as the screen is stuck."* The weight row on Log › Health is inline, not a
sheet, so the fix could not have touched it — but the weigh-in sheet from History and the drink
sheet were both z-50 and both broken, and the installed app only reloads onto a new build on the
next open after Cloudflare has built it. Asked which screen and for a screenshot; before either
came: *"Seems to work now ?"* — the phone had picked up v85. Lesson for the next report that
arrives within minutes of a release: ask whether the app has been reopened since, before reading
it as a new bug.

Fix of a broken screen, so merged to `main` without asking (CLAUDE.md, *When to merge without
asking*).

## BTL-B129 — on iOS the sheets were placed by `<main>`, not the viewport

Ben, 09:05, three screenshots on the v85 build: *"Actually the edit history screen for yesterday's
climbing sesh still stuck. It looks like it's scrolling in the background but the screen doesn't
move."* The weigh-in sheet worked; the session sheet did not.

**What the screenshots show, read again.** The header and the tabs are not dimmed. On v85 that
cannot be paint order — the rows have no z-index now and the backdrop covers them in Chromium —
so the backdrop itself must stop at the header and the tabs: the overlay is the size of `<main>`,
not the viewport. A 92vh sheet in a box the height of `<main>` overflows it at both ends and is
clipped, title and footer gone; a short sheet fits and works, which is exactly the weigh-in sheet.
The scroll indicator on the right is the History page scrolling under a sheet that does not move.

**Cause.** Since BTL-B127 the sheets render inside `<main>`, the scroller, and on iOS WebKit the
scroller was acting as the containing block for `position: fixed` descendants — the behaviour
`-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch` was known for, and the style B127 put on `<main>`. Chromium
does not do this, which is why neither the B127 harness run nor this morning's saw it. B128 was a
real fault and stays fixed; it was not the whole fault.

**Fix.** Structural, so it holds whatever iOS does with a scroller: `components/ui/Layer.jsx`
portals its children into `document.body`, and every overlay in the app renders inside one — the
22 roots at `fixed inset-0`: session detail, climb / hangboard editors, gym, cardio, drink and
weigh-in sheets, the hangboard timer, exercise and routine modals, the confirm dialog, goals,
friends, help, settings, locations, delete account, the comp entry sheet and the board sheet. A
portal to the body is outside the shell, so it is placed against the viewport and paints after the
shell in tree order. `<main>` drops `-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch` (a no-op on iOS 13+ at best,
this at worst). The comp tabs, the Plan FAB and the Log toast stay as they are: `fixed` with offsets
inside `<main>`, visible either way.

**Checked** that no sheet sits under an element with an `onClick` — React events bubble through a
portal along the React tree — the hits near each render site are sibling buttons, not ancestors.

**Seen running** in Chromium at 390 × 844 with Firebase faked: every overlay is a child of
`document.body`; the session sheet's Edit and Delete hit-test to themselves; Edit opens the climb
editor over it (the sheet's buttons blocked by the editor, as they should be) and the editor's X
returns to the sheet; Delete arms; X closes; the weigh-in sheet's Save and Delete reachable;
Settings opens, Locations stacks over it. Build, 937 tests, lint clean. Cache v85 → v86.

**Not seen on a phone.** The check is the same sheet: title and X at the top, Edit at the bottom,
header and tabs dimmed behind it.

Fix of a broken screen, merged to `main` without asking.

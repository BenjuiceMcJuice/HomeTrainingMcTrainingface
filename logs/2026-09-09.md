# 2026-09-09

## Alcohol widget — denser milestone ladder + lively motion

Ben asked for the alcohol widget to feel more dynamic as it progresses through its
goals, with the milestones firing more often. Options were put to him first; he
picked the "lively" dial, said to leave the chart alone, and asked for the specific
effects to be a surprise.

**Files changed:** `betalog-react/src/components/dashboard/AlcoholFreeCard.jsx` (only).
`BarTimeline` is untouched, so cardio and the level cards are unaffected.

### The ladder — six rungs to twelve

`TIERS` was 7 / 14 / 30 / 60 / 90 / 180. That left a month of nothing between 30 and
60 and three months of nothing between 90 and 180 — the reward schedule went quiet
exactly where the streak gets hard. Now:

7 · 14 · **21** · 30 · **45** · 60 · 90 · **120** · **150** · 180 · **270** · **365**

Weekly to three weeks, fortnightly to two months, monthly to six, then the long ones.
New rungs get their own palette, icon and label in the existing house style
(Sprout / Sparkles / Award / Medal / Gem / Sun). Every tier also gained a `short`
name, used when it is the *next* rung.

`grindNote` (the in-between copy) was extended to the 14, 21, 30, 45 and 60 rungs —
previously only the 7 rung had it, so days 14–29 had nothing to say. Two new
`GROWTH_FACTS` entries for 270 and 365.

### Motion

Kept inside CSS keyframes — no animation library, nothing added to the bundle.
Everything scales with where the streak is: the day a rung is *cleared* is treated
as a distinct event from the days spent standing on it (`isMilestoneDay`), and past
90 days the card is never completely still.

Also new: a hairline progress line under the week strip reading
"N days to <next rung>", which fills towards the target on open. With twelve rungs
there is nearly always one in view, and it is what makes the denser ladder legible.

### Reduced motion

The card previously ignored `prefers-reduced-motion` entirely — it was, and is, the
only component in the app that defines keyframes. Now a media query kills every
animation and transition inside `.al-card`, and the purely decorative layers
(`.al-sheen`) are removed outright rather than frozen mid-sweep — a stopped sheen is
a stray band across the card. The count-up checks `matchMedia` and starts at its
final value.

### Verified

`npm run build`, `npm test` (334 pass) and `npm run lint` (0 errors; no findings in
the changed file) all clean. Rendered at 18 streak lengths — 0, 1, 4, 6, 7, 9, 14,
16, 21, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 270, 365 — in headless Chromium via a
throwaway preview harness (deleted; the real app needs Firebase auth). No console
errors. Two things the screenshots caught and fixed: the progress track used the
tier border at full strength, so an empty track read as a *finished* bar on the
amber tiers; and the sheen sat visible mid-card under reduced motion.

### Known, not changed

At 45–59 days the headline says "1 month" while the tier label says "six weeks" —
`streak.months` is `floor(days / 30)`. Pre-existing headline logic; the new 45 rung
just makes it visible. Left alone rather than quietly changing a headline nobody
asked about.

## Hangboard UX improvements

### GripDiagram component (`src/components/log/GripDiagram.jsx`) — new file
- Two-panel SVG (230×80) shown above the countdown during a hang routine
- Left panel: top-down finger silhouettes (pill shapes) — active fingers highlighted, inactive dimmed; bottom corner radius varies by grip type (open = rounder, full crimp = almost square)
- Right panel: iconic side-profile shape per grip type — "I" drag, slight-hook open hand, "7" half crimp, "Λ" full crimp with knuckle above the hold
- Accent dot at the bend point for half/full crimp
- Fingertips anchored ON the hold bar (left panel starts at y=6, right panel contact points at y=10)
- Full crimp knuckle dot at y=2 — just above the hold, which is anatomically correct

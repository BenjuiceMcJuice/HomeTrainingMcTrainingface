## Training review off the JSON export

Ben asked for a review of the last few weeks, first week back post-holiday. Cloud
sessions have no Firestore access — no `firebase`/`gcloud` CLI, no login token, and
unauthenticated Firestore REST returns 403 — so the review ran off a Settings →
Data → Export JSON dump he pasted in. Worth remembering for next time: the export
is the route, not the database.

Findings that matter to the app rather than the training:

- **`attempts` never increments.** All 54 climbs ever logged carry `attempts: 1`,
  including 7 marked `sent` and 3 marked `attempt`. The AI coach is being fed a
  flat field, so it can never see projecting.
- **Walk durations sit on the 30-minute default.** 24 of 36 walks are exactly
  30 min regardless of distance — 5.3 mi in 30 min is 10.6 mph. Since the MET calc
  is duration-based, most walk calorie figures are understated by roughly half, and
  the cardio widget inherits it.

Neither is fixed here. Both are logged for a later pass.

## Shameometer — scoring the schedule you ignored

Ben's ask: flag missed scheduled routines, with a gauge "from great to bad".

Every existing widget summarises what *was* logged, so a routine could sit in the
schedule firing reminders for weeks and never surface anywhere as a failure. His own
data is the case for it: three schedule entries, 37 due days in the last 30, zero
done.

**New files:** `src/lib/adherence.js`, `src/lib/__tests__/adherence.test.js` (26
tests), `src/components/dashboard/ShameometerCard.jsx`.
**Touched:** `Dashboard.jsx` (import, `DEFAULT_ORDER`, render case), `WidgetPicker.jsx`
(option + cap), `widgetWindow.js` (chips + default), `widgetCollapse.js` (comment only).

### The three scoring rules

These are the whole feature; the arithmetic is trivial beside them.

1. **Today is never missed.** The window ends *yesterday*. A routine due today is
   still in play, and counting it would drag every score down each morning and
   recover it each evening — noise, not signal.
2. **Nothing before `remindFrom` counts.** That field already holds the first
   scheduled day at the time the reminder was set, so it is the honest start of the
   commitment. Without it, adding a routine today opens with weeks of retroactive
   failure for days when nothing was promised.
3. **Default routines match by family, not by versioned id.** Seeded routines carry
   a version suffix and a session stores whichever id was current when it was logged.
   Ben's schedule points at `dr-submaxrepeaters-v5` while all three of his hangboard
   sessions are `dr-submaxrepeaters-v4` — a strict id match scores real sessions as
   zero and reports "never done". `routineFamily()` strips a trailing `-vN`, and only
   from `dr-` ids so user uuids are untouched.

Also: the overall percentage **pools due days** rather than averaging the per-routine
rates. A routine scheduled 7×/week is a bigger commitment than a Monday one, and
averaging lets the Monday one paper over it — on Ben's data pooling gives 0% where
averaging would flatter it.

`scored: false` when nothing was due in the window (new schedule, or one whose
weekday hasn't come round), so the card says so instead of showing a 0% nobody
earned. The card renders nothing at all when the schedule is empty.

### The gauge

`SHAME_BANDS`, eight rungs, best first: Flawless 95 · Locked in 85 · Solid 70 ·
Wobbly 55 · Patchy 40 · Shaky 20 · Ghosting it 1 · Total shame 0. Deliberately
uneven — the top is split finely because the difference between 90% and 100% is
where the habit lives, while everything under 40% is the same message told louder.
Tone is the only thing in the array, so retuning it is a one-file edit.

Body is a tinted meter bar, the band's note, a per-routine breakdown sorted
worst-first, and a "Worst: X · N in a row missed · last done Y" line. A percentage
alone is arguable; a date is not.

### Config

- Windows `30d` / `90d`, defaulting to **30d** — this is an "are you doing it *now*"
  card and a quarter lets a good month hide a bad fortnight. No `12m`: `remindFrom`
  truncates the window anyway, and on Ben's data 30d and 90d already return the
  identical 0/37.
- Not in `COLLAPSE_DEFAULTS`, so it arrives expanded. It has no chart, and a card
  whose job is to be uncomfortable does not get to arrive pre-folded.
- `MAX_WIDGETS` 10 → 12. Ten options against a cap of ten would render every chip
  as at-limit the moment they were all on.

## Weekly Shameometer — spec written, not built

Ben's follow-up: he wants a **weekly** score on a needle dial (VERY POOR → EXCELLENT,
per a reference image), improved by scheduled routines *and* any other training, and
knocked down by drinking. Asked to spec before building.

Written to `docs/specs/betalog_shameometer_spec.md`. Headlines:

- **Training 75 / Schedule 25 / Alcohol −25…+10.** Training is the backbone because
  the adherence-only card reads 0 for every week Ben has ever had — a gauge with one
  value is not a gauge.
- **Effort points** by session type (climb/hang 3, gym/swim/sport 2, walk 1), capped
  at 4/day, target 9/week. Without weighting, three walks would score like three
  climbing sessions.
- **Deliberately no calories or duration** in the score — the walk-duration default
  bug makes every duration-derived figure unreliable. Scoring by session type is
  immune to it.
- **Pro-rated mid-week** with the elapsed-day divisor floored at 3, so Monday isn't
  automatically VERY POOR and a single walk on day 1 isn't EXCELLENT.
- **Calibrated against all 15 weeks in the export** before any number went into the
  spec. Spread lands across all five bands; holiday weeks bottom out; the current
  week reads FAIR (60), held down by 0/17 on the schedule.

`lib/adherence.js` survives untouched as the schedule component. `ShameometerCard`
gets replaced by the dial. Four open questions left for Ben at the end of the spec.

Nothing built. Awaiting sign-off on the weighting.

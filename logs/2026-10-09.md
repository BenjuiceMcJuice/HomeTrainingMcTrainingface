## BTL-B126 — Repeat replaces Project

Ben, with a Dashboard screenshot: *"For climbs logged we have attempt and project. They're basically
the same thing? I was thinking having 'repeat' as an option maybe in-between send and attempt. Eg
today I did a V4 then did it again. I wanna log it but cos I had the moves dialled it felt easier."*
Then, on the recommendation to drop Project rather than keep five buttons: *"Drop and replace.
Build and merge."*

**Why.** Project and Attempt were one outcome under two labels — the guide said so (*"Same as an
attempt for the maths"*), and nothing in stats, pyramid or goals read `project` as its own thing.
Project was also a claim about intent, which the data-honesty spec rules out. Repeat, by contrast,
closes a real honesty hole: without it, sending the same V4 twice logged two V4 sends, and eight laps
of one problem would have owned the grade. The pyramid spec had called this *"the one real gap"*
(§3) since September and settled for no cap because Ben said he would not log repeats; now he can,
and the pyramid ignores them.

**The rule.** A repeat is a clean ascent of a problem already sent. It is a *climb* wherever the
session is described — the climb count, History's *Best* and level pill, the widget bars (a faint
accent segment after the sends, with its own legend entry), the friends feed's *N sent*, the
coach's climb line (*1x V4 repeat (already sent)*). It is never a *send* wherever a send is
evidence of grade — pyramid credit, send-goal evidence, *Best* on the level card, the public profile's
highest send. Two helpers in `stats.js` say which is which: `isSend` (flashed, sent) and `isClean`
(flashed, sent, repeat).

**Files.** `ClimbLogger.jsx` (the buttons, Repeat in a dusky blue between Send and Attempt),
`SessionDetailSheet.jsx`, `stats.js` (`repeats` per grade and in total; `hardestGrade` reads clean
ascents), `pyramid.js` (`repeats` per tier, never credited), `GradeChart.jsx`, `coach.js`,
`storage.js` (`project` → `attempt` on load, beside `fell`), `types.js`, the guide and the
explainer, the data model, the pyramid spec (§3, decisions 14 and 16, the cap note) and the
logging-as-you-go spec. Tests: fixtures that used `project` now use `attempt`; new cases in
pyramid, stats, sessions, goals, coach and a new `storageOutcome.test.js` for the load remap —
937 pass. The coach's fixed terminology was left alone: a first attempt to explain *repeat* there
broke the "summary is cheaper than full" test by a few characters, so the explanation rides the
climb line only when a repeat occurs.

**Seen running** in Chromium at 390 px with Firebase faked (`dev/`): a session of V4 send, two V4
repeats, V3 flash and send, V5 attempt and V5 repeat — the pyramid reads V4 1/8 and V5 0/8, the bars
V4 1/3 and V5 0/2 with the repeat segments drawn, History *7 climbs · Best V5*. Build, lint clean.
Cache v82 → v83.

**Not done in this one.** The nav in Ben's screenshot was floating mid-page again, the third time
(B44, B101). Opened as BTL-B127 with the structural fix proposed; built next, below.

Merged to `main` on Ben's word (*"Build and merge."*).

## BTL-B127 — the tabs are a row of the shell, not a layer over the page

Ben: *"Build the nav fix on a branch and merge like a good man."*

**Cause, properly.** B44 (blur dropped, 18 Sept) and B101 (scroll nudged after the keyboard, 27 Sept)
each covered one trigger of one cause: the document scrolled, and the tabs were `position: fixed`
over it. iOS WebKit paints a fixed element at a stale document offset whenever the document's
height changes under it, and a Dashboard has a dozen ways to change height — a widget collapsing,
the 90d / All time toggle, a sheet closing, the keyboard. The third screenshot had no keyboard in
it. Patching triggers one at a time was never going to end.

**Fix.** The document no longer scrolls. `App.jsx` wraps everything in `.app-shell` — `100dvh`
with a `100vh` fallback (`index.css`) — a flex column of the header, `<main id="app-scroll">`
(`flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-y-auto`) and `BottomTabs`, a named export from `Nav.jsx` rendered after
`<main>`. The tabs are an ordinary last row; the header and the desktop nav lose `sticky` (nothing
moves under them now) and their blur (nothing to see through); the mobile header takes
`env(safe-area-inset-top)` since it is the top of the shell. `html, body` get
`overscroll-behavior-y: none` so a drag at the edge does not bounce a document that cannot scroll.

Everything that scrolled the window scrolls the scroller: `scrollToTop()` in `lib/utils.js`,
used by `ScrollToTop` on route change and by the logger's Done / Save. `useViewportSettle` keeps
its job — the keyboard can still scroll the *window* on iOS to reveal a field — but its action is
now `window.scrollTo(0, 0)`, the shell's only correct position, instead of a pixel nudge of a fixed
layer that no longer exists. Pages drop `min-h-screen pb-24` for `min-h-full pb-8` (the clearance
was for a bar that floated; the minimum height was a viewport, which inside a scroller left a
blank tab-bar's worth at the bottom); Coach's `h-[calc(100dvh-120px)]` becomes `h-full`;
`CompLayout` is `min-h-full`. The comp tabs stay `fixed` — each comp page renders them, so they
cannot be a shell row — and the comment says why that is safe now: nothing scrolls under them.

**Seen running** in Chromium with Firebase faked, 390 × 844 and 1100 × 700: the document is never
scrollable (`scrollHeight === innerHeight`, `scrollY` 0 throughout), `main` scrolls 273 px on a
seeded Dashboard, the tabs sit on the bottom edge scrolled, after a widget collapse and after a
route change, the route change resets `main` to the top, Coach and the comp home render inside the
shell. Build, 937 tests, lint clean. Cache v83 → v84.

**Not seen on a phone.** Neither were B44 or B101. The test that matters is Ben's: open the
Dashboard, toggle 90d / All time, collapse a widget, open and close a sheet, type in a field and
dismiss the keyboard. If the bar still moves, the cause is something else and a screenshot is the
evidence.

Merged to `main` on Ben's word.

## BTL-B49 — the Own forecast's rate at the target is a blend

Ben, on the options for the second-send jump: *"Explore both using maths charts to work it
out … would the average climber's rate of improvement follow a pattern, ie linear, or might it
flatten as the grades go up? Have a good research, do some maths and come back with options."*
Then: *"Go with F. Build it please. Maybe summarise how this works in the explainer section
also."*

**The research** (write-up with charts: <https://claude.ai/artifact/T4YjhVE1T4Evz4zxyWc1Ak>).
Masullo's scrape of 4 million 8a.nu ascents (62,593 climbers): about a grade a year for the
first three years, half a grade by year four, under a quarter after six; 7a→7b ≈ 1.5 years,
7b→8a ≈ 2.5 among those who got there — so 1.1–1.4× longer per grade in BetaLog's band, not
the 2× of folklore, and the dramatic flattening is people plateauing at 7c+/8a and stopping.
Send *frequency* is the steep curve: theCrag has ~5 ascents at a grade per 1 at the next,
8a.nu's rule is 3–4 per letter grade, and Ben's own 90-day chart reads 8 · 5 · 2 · 1 from
6a+ to 6c. The pyramid's 8·4·2·1 is that curve. (The proxy blocked the full articles; the
numbers are from search excerpts.)

**The maths.** `sim.py` (scratch, not in the repo): 150 climbers built to look like Ben's log
on 18 September, sending at a rate that halves per half-grade above their ability, improving
one half-grade per 60 days and slowing by 1.4× per half-grade (also flat, 2×, 120-day and
a climber climbing 2.5× as often). Each week six options forecast the day they own 6c:

| | 2nd-send jump | median error (slowing) | Ben, 2 sends |
|---|---|---|---|
| A live | +217 d | +36 | mid-March 2027 |
| B from first send | +66 | +8 | mid-December |
| C blend with base rate | −33 | −1 | early November |
| D pyramid prior, no improvement | −103 | +50 | late April 2027 |
| E steps × pace | +1 | +3 | mid-March 2027 |
| F blend with base rate halved | −40 | +4 | late November |

Truth ≈ 140 days out (early February 2027). The base rate standing in — three rows of easier
sends summed — was four to eight times too fast, so the measured reading looked like a leap
when it was the nearer of the two. F is best or joint-best in every scenario and every send
brings its date nearer. Ben chose F.

**Built.**
- `pyramid.js` — `byGrade[grade].firstSend`, the earliest send date at each grade in the window.
- `pyramidForecast.js` — `targetRatio(readiness)`: median of each base row's sends over the
  row below (lower ≥ 2, upper ≥ 1), clamped 0.3–0.8, `DEFAULT_TARGET_RATIO` 0.5 when thin.
  The own step: `assumed = rate.perDay × ratio`; `ownSpan` from `firstSend`, floored at
  `MIN_RATE_DAYS`; `perDay = (credit + MIN_TARGET_SENDS) / (ownSpan + MIN_TARGET_SENDS /
  assumed)`. `own.source` is `assumed` / `blend`; `own.ratio`, `own.assumedPerDay`,
  `own.spanDays` carried for the words. Steps line: *…then 7 more 6c sends (about 9 weeks,
  from your 1 in 28 days, steadied by assuming 6c comes about half as often as the rows
  below)*; before a send, *assuming 6c comes about half as often as the rows below until you
  have sent it*. `looseRatio` says a third / half / three-quarters.
- `pyramid.html` step 3 rewritten. Cache v40 → v41. Spec §9a step 3, decision 15, sources.
- Tests: assumed rate and wording, ratio from shaped / top-heavy / steep / thin rows, the
  blend and its wording, monotone across 0→1→2→3 sends with no send moving the date more
  than six weeks, the four-week floor on a flurry, the year in the headline on a thin log.
  The `at(twoV5)` fixture is top-heavy (8 V4 over 4 V3) so it reads *three-quarters*.

**Verified.** build, 732 tests, lint clean. Not yet on a phone — BTL-B57.

## Flashpoint is not a chip, and the pin looks dead

Ben, with the logger open at home: *"I've been to flashpoint before but it's not showing
against the options and no obvious location functionally working. Pressing the button does
nothing. Thoughts?"* Then: *"Do what you think is best."*

**Why.** `profile.venues` grew only from saves made since the feature landed on 18 September,
and `nearbyVenues` dropped any venue without coordinates, so Flashpoint — climbed at before
that day — was not in the list, and could not have been a chip even if it were. Editing never
attached a fix (`!editing` in `posForVenue`) and never auto-located, so the Edit Session pin
was grey. His second screenshot shows the pin *did* work: it turned orange and the grey line
said *Nothing saved near here yet* — the whole of the feedback, indistinguishable from nothing.

**Built.**
- `lib/venues.js` — `venuesFromSessions(sessions)`: one venue per distinct location text
  (session's `location`, else the first climb's), session count, latest date, most recent
  spelling. `mergeVenues(saved, fromSessions)`: by name key; coordinates and spelling from the
  saved entry, the larger count and later date; either side alone is kept. `recentVenues`:
  the first `RECENT_CHIPS` (5). `sessionLocation` is the same fallback the logger's
  `seedLocation` uses.
- `hooks/useVenues.js` — `venues` is now the merge, memoised on `[profile.venues,
  data.sessions]`; `rememberVenue` and `locatedBefore` still work off the saved array.
- `components/log/VenuePicker.jsx` — a `recent` prop; the chips are `nearby` when any, else
  `recent` without a distance. The notes: *Located — none of your venues is within 300 m.
  Your recent ones are here instead* / *Located — nothing saved within 300 m yet…*; the denied
  and no-fix lines mention the recent chips.
- `components/log/ClimbLogger.jsx` — `sameDayEdit`: an edit of a session dated today
  auto-locates and attaches the fix like a new session. `posForVenue` drops `!editing`.
- Cache v41 → v42. Data model spec: the `venues` paragraph says where the names come from.
- Tests: 12 new in `venues.test.js` — the log's venues, the fallback to climbs, the spelling
  kept, the merge each way, no mutation, and Flashpoint becoming a nearby chip after one save
  with a fix. 744 pass.

**Verified.** build, tests, lint (0 errors, the 13 standing warnings). The picker rendered in
Chromium in all four states from a throwaway harness (idle, located with nothing near, located
at Redpoint with *36 m*, denied). Not yet on a phone — BTL-B58. Stopped at the branch, as a
screen that already worked had changed; Ben: *"The word"* — merged and released the same day.

**Open.** BTL-B59 — a session dated today but saved from home stamps home's coordinates on the
venue. The rule was always exposed to that; a recent chip makes the save a tap, so likelier now.

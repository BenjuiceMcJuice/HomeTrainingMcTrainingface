## Settings sheet could not be closed on a phone

**What it was.** Not the Admin page — the Settings sheet. Its panel is a fixed, bottom-anchored
overlay with no height cap and no scroll of its own. Every row added to it this month — the Groq
key when AI Coach is on, Beep timing (2026-09-17), the build line (2026-09-13), the Admin panel
link — made it taller, and on a phone it passed the height of the screen. A bottom-anchored panel
taller than its container overflows off the *top*, so the header with the X was above the screen
and unreachable. The scrollbar Ben saw was the page underneath: the panel covers the whole
backdrop, so nothing he touched scrolled or closed the sheet. Only *Admin panel* and *Sign out*
still led anywhere.

Reproduced in a throwaway harness at 390 × 844 with AI Coach on and the admin row showing: the
panel was 957 px tall in an 844 px viewport, its top edge at −113 px, the X at −97 px. Playwright
could not click it.

**Fix.** `max-h-[85vh] overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain` on the panel — the same three classes
the friends sheet already had. The panel now stops at 85 % of the screen and scrolls inside
itself; the header is on screen when the sheet opens, and the X closes it. Same harness after
the change: panel 717 px, top at 127 px, X clicked and the sheet closed.

Files: `src/components/layout/SettingsSheet.jsx` (one line). Build, tests (730) and lint clean.
Broken control, so merged and released without asking, per the rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
